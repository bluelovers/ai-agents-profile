### 【東方寺院・多段落敘事】祈願幡庭院

> 視平線寬景：長袍背影立於石鋪庭院中央的青銅香爐前，頭頂垂掛成片黃紅祈願幡填滿畫面上半，竹葉與傳統瓦簷從兩側透出，剪影人物散布前後景——分「主體→天幕→細節→群像→風格→光線→氛圍」七段推進。
> ⚠ 開頭 `velnari` 是模型／LoRA 的**觸發詞**（非場景描述），換模型時應移除或替換。

```
velnari
A wide eye-level shot reveals a stone-paved courtyard where a solitary figure in long, flowing robes stands at the center, facing a large, ornate bronze incense burner with their back to the viewer.
Above them, an overwhelming canopy of countless yellow and red prayer ribbons hangs vertically from the dark wooden beams of an overhead structure, creating a dense, colorful curtain that fills the upper half of the frame.
Green bamboo leaves peek through the ribbons on the left and right, and a traditional tiled roof is barely visible behind the hanging banners.
Thin wisps of incense smoke rise from the burner, drifting upward into the light.

Several other dark, silhouetted figures stand or walk through the courtyard in the foreground and background, their forms blurred and indistinct, reading as quiet presences within the scene.

The scene is rendered in a painterly, illustrative style with soft brushstrokes.
The color palette is warm and golden, dominated by the brilliant yellow of the ribbons, the deep reds of the accents, the dark browns of the architecture, and the soft greens of the bamboo.

Strong warm light filters down from above, illuminating the hanging ribbons and balancing the cool shaded tones beneath the beams, casting long, soft shadows on the stone floor.
The overall mood is one of quiet reverence, spiritual stillness, and ancient tradition.
```
