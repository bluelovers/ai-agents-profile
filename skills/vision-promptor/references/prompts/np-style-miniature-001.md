# 微縮 3D 城市範例 (Miniature Isometric City)

## 分類索引

---

### 【微縮・等角 3D】台北市天氣資訊卡

> 45° 等角俯視的微縮 3D 卡通城市：以台北市地標與建築元素為主體，柔和 PBR 材質與真實光影，
> 並將「當天天氣」直接整合進城市環境；畫面頂部置中疊加標題「台北市」、天氣圖示、日期與溫度——
> 示範「場景生成＋資訊圖表排版」的複合需求（文字位置、字級、對齊方式皆需明確指定）。

```
Present a clear, 45° top-down isometric miniature 3D cartoon scene of 台北市,
featuring its most iconic landmarks and architectural elements.
Use soft, refined textures with realistic PBR materials and gentle, lifelike lighting and shadows. Integrate the current weather conditions directly into the city environment to create an immersive atmospheric mood.
Use a clean, minimalistic composition with a soft, solid-colored background.
At the top-center, place the title “台北市” in large bold text,
a prominent weather icon beneath it, then the date (small text）and temperature (medium text).
All text must be centered with consistent spacing, and may subtly overlap the tops of the buildings.
```

> ⚠ 文字層要求（標題、圖示、日期、溫度）依賴模型的文字渲染能力——
> 中文大字尤易出錯，必要時可改為生成後合成文字。
