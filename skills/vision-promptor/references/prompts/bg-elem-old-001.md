# 背景與環境要素範例 (Background & Environment Elements)

## 分類索引

- **【室內・居所】** — 土黃灰泥牆與木窗、粉色床帳房間（第一人稱）
- **【自然・水景】** — 湖畔度假营地、河岸草地
- **【自然・林道】** — 森林石徑與指標柱
- **【夜景・氛圍】** — 日本鄉間螢火蟲
- **【奇幻・室內】** — 地下溫室蘭花藤蔓
- **【片段・單句】** — 超市貨架（簡易片段）

---

### 【室內・居所】土黃灰泥牆與木窗

> 粗糙白灰泥牆＋鼠尾草綠木窗，長影與重顆粒膠片質感，色調去飽和、黑階上提——示範「以材質與色盤定義室內氛圍」。

```
inside a rustic stucco building, focusing on a thick whitewashed plaster wall and a sage-green painted casement window.
long shadows, and visible heavy film grain.
The color palette features desaturated sage green, warm ochre, and muted earth tones with lifted, milky-grey blacks, warm-yellow color cast, and soft focus falling off around the edges of the frame.
```

### 【室內・居所】粉色床帳房間（第一人稱）

> 第一人稱俯視床帳內部：粉色格子被、圓枕、帳頂鐵管、倒掛兔玩偶、荷葉邊小燈、狗掛毯、白貓玩偶與耳機——以密集的物件清單鋪陳個人化小空間，暖粉燈光收尾。

```
第一人称在粉色床帐里往下看.
盖着白布的一角.床上是粉色格子被和圆垫.
四周和顶上包着粉色布, 铁管沿着帐顶.灰色吊扇, 一只灰白兔子玩偶倒挂在顶上.一盏白色荷叶边小灯亮着.
床尾挂着大耳白狗的挂毯, 旁边是颈枕, 小玩偶和一副挂着的耳机.
白色小桌板上摊着一本打开的书.右侧挂着格子收纳袋, 上面一只蓝衣服白猫玩偶.左侧是粉色纱帘, 帘外是黑的.
暖粉色灯光, 写实.
```

### 【自然・水景】湖畔度假營地

> 攝影機貼水面 10 公分的低視角：沙岸、老舊木棧橋、薄荷綠與象牙色琺瑯躺椅、對岸松樺林倒影——2000 年代中歐度假營懷舊氛圍，廣景深。

```
lakefront in a nostalgic forest holiday camp, camera at waterline, 10 cm above sand, immersive low view toward pier, a small sandy beach and weathered wooden pier reaching into calm lake water, faded mint and ivory enamel loungers, distant pine and birch forest mirrored on the far shore, sand sloping into shallow water and pier planks, golden afternoon sunlight, faded mint and warm sand accents, early 2000s Central European holiday atmosphere, weathered wood,
wide depth of field,
```

### 【自然・林道】森林石徑與指標柱

> 建立鏡頭：S 形石徑在松樹與白樺間蜿蜒，蕨類沿邊、木製指標柱立於彎道旁，午後陽光斜穿林霧——同一度假營的陸上場景，與上一則同屬一組。

```
a winding stone path deep inside a picturesque forest holiday camp, wide establishing view of a generous S-shaped trail curving between tall pine trunks and white birches, flat irregular stone slabs set in packed earth, lush ferns along grassy verges, weathered sage wooden direction post with plain arrow shapes beside the bend, golden late-afternoon sun shafts crossing the trail through fine woodland haze, fresh fern-green accents and warm amber highlights, nostalgic early 2000s Central European holiday atmosphere, weathered wood, warm film-photo tones, wide depth of field
```

### 【夜景・氛圍】日本鄉間螢火蟲

> 極簡兩句的夜景氛圍：遠處燈火明滅、田野延展、螢火蟲飛舞——示範「短提示也能成立的氛圍寫法」。

```
Faint lights dance about in the nighttime in rural japanese landscape, The countryside stretches out, and fireflies are flitting about.
```

### 【自然・水景】河岸草地（背面視角）

> 背面視角的寬闊河岸：右側巨樹幹與頂部落葉框景、陽光穿葉灑下光斑、對岸深綠森林——以豐富綠色層次與暖陽營造靜謐親密感。

```
on a grassy riverbank, viewed from behind. wide river that reflects the surrounding greenery.
The scene is framed by the massive, thick trunk of a tree on the right and a dense canopy of vibrant green leaves that stretches across the top and down the left side, dappled with small red flowers or berries. Sunlight filters through the leaves, creating bright patches of light on the lush grass and the water. On the left, a white piece of clothing or cloth lies on the ground. In the distance, the opposite bank is covered in thick, dark green forest.
The overall palette is dominated by rich, varied greens, with warm sunlight creating a peaceful, intimate, and serene atmosphere
```

### 【奇幻・室內】地下溫室蘭花藤蔓

> 標籤式寫法：半透明蘭花脈絡、水晶托盤、地下溫室、大理石柱上的發光藤蔓、地面霧氣與懸浮花粉——示範「密集意象堆疊出奇幻植物空間」。

```
translucent orchid veins,
carrying a crystal tray with dew-filled blossoms, in vast underground greenhouse, bioluminescent vines coiled around marble columns, fog hovering just above floor, glowing pollen particles suspended in air, pastel ambient tones, stylized floral fractals, serene botanical symmetry, scenery
```

### 【片段・單句】超市貨架（簡易片段）

> ⚠ 僅一句、缺主語與場景脈絡——**屬簡易片段**。

```
depicting a shelves in a supermarket.
```
