# 流暢敘事段落式範例 (Fluent Narrative Paragraph Examples)

> 範例下一則編號由 **P0009** 開始，空號不補。
> 長提示依語意分行、段間空行——排版規則見 [np-fluent-narrative-paragraph.md](../np-fluent-narrative-paragraph.md)。

## 分類索引

**依題材分類：**

- **奇幻／地下** — P0002 地下洞穴暗精靈城市、P0003 暗精靈王座與金門
- **科幻／宇宙** — P0004 荒蕪行星地表、P0006 氣體巨行星浮空都市、P0008 環帶行星與冰晶碎塊
- **未來都市** — P0007 雲海平台獨影與巨環
- **生活／寫實** — P0001 陽台晾衣繩、P0005 雪夜路燈

> ⚠ P0002、P0003 的暗精靈屬**場景／建築**範例——
> 角色外型模組（可插入插槽的種族描述）另收於 [np-char-demi-human.md](np-char-demi-human.md)。

> ⚠️ **標題中的「多段落長敘事／單段緊湊敘事／分行長敘事／標籤串列型」等標註，描述的是現況而非建議格式**——
> 這些其實都只是「**沒有進行適當分行與空行**」的變體，並非不同的寫法流派。
> 實務上建立新提示詞時，請養成良好的排版習慣：**避免單行過長**——過長時在**句子邊界**分行，
> 也可依**語句的主題性**分行或分段（換主體、換空間區域時另起一行／段）
> （逗號不是維持同行的判斷標準，過長時仍需分行維持可讀性），但也**不要無差別地把全部都分行**（短行維持原狀即可）。
> 段與段之間空一行；若提示詞編輯器會自動吞掉空行，以 `#` 保留空行，必要時以 `# ---` 作為分隔線。
> 完整排版規則見 [np-fluent-narrative-paragraph.md](../np-fluent-narrative-paragraph.md) 的「排版規則（分行與空行）」。

### P0002. 地下洞穴暗精靈城市（多段落長敘事）

```
cinematic, rich colors, deep beneath the crust of the world lies a cavern so vast it feels like another sky. Its ceiling rises into darkness, lost beyond towering stalactites that glow faintly with veins of blue crystal. The entire cavern is illuminated by an eerie, shimmering light that pulses from thousands of crystal clusters embedded in the walls, casting shifting reflections across a great underground lake of deep blue water. The lake stretches across the cavern floor like a mirror of liquid sapphire, its surface rippling with soft waves that echo endlessly.

Along the shores of this luminous lake, an immense dark elf city unfolds in layers carved directly into the cavern walls. Entire terraces of obsidian stone and dark metal rise in dramatic stacked tiers, each connected by spiraling walkways, chain-lifts, and suspended bridges reinforced with enchanted ironwork. Tall slender towers rise from the terraces, their spires tipped with crystal lanterns that glow with cold blue fire. Some structures curve like blades, others like twisting roots, merging architecture and natural stone in seamless, elegant forms.

At the heart of the city stands the Dark Elf Castle: an enormous fortress of black marble and onyx, crowned with spires that resemble spears piercing upward into the cavern's shadowed heights. The walls are etched with silver runes that faintly pulse, connecting the castle to the energy of the crystals embedded in the cavern. A massive gate of dark metal and gold filigree guards the entrance, its surface carved with scenes of ancient triumphs and hidden tragedies. Above the castle, colossal chains anchor parts of its highest towers to the cavern ceiling, stabilizing the structure like a suspended citadel.

Below the castle, at the edge of the lake, lies a sprawling medieval-style port. Wooden docks stretch into the glowing water, lit by floating crystal lanterns tethered by fine chains. Dark elf sailors move in graceful formation, guiding sleek, sharp-prowed boats through the water. These vessels are crafted of lacquered black wood reinforced with gleaming metal ribs, their sails woven from shimmering spider-silk dyed in deep indigo. Some boats glide silently, propelled by oars shaped like blade wings, while others are driven by faintly humming crystal engines that churn with blue light beneath their hulls.

The port bustles quietly, its activity muted by the vastness of the cavern. Crates of supplies are unloaded onto stone platforms where mechanical lifts carry goods up toward higher tiers of the city. Looming cranes made of black metal and crystal pivot slowly, their arms moving with precision as they transfer massive loads. Sparks crackle from forges recessed into the cavern wall, their flames blue and unnatural, casting harsh shadows onto the workers below.

Everywhere, the cavern breathes with a mixture of ancient magic and industrial might. Crystal light reflects off the water, bouncing onto the stone architecture in ghostly patterns. The air hums with quiet energy, a rhythm of machinery, magic, and life. The city feels both eternal and alive, a hidden world where darkness is not absence but power, where elegance and danger exist in the same breath.

This underground realm is a kingdom unto itself: vast, secret, and breathtaking in its depth and complexity. A place of shadows and brilliance, of stillness and motion, where the dark elves rule with quiet majesty beneath the sleeping world above.
```

### P0003. 暗精靈王座與金門（多段落長敘事）

```
cinematic, rich colors, deep beneath the earth, an immense subterranean chamber unfolds like a hidden throne room carved into the bones of the world. Towering walls of black stone rise upward until they disappear into darkness, their surfaces embedded with clusters of glowing crystals that pulse with soft violet and blue light. Their radiance spills across the cavern, catching on veins of silver and faint trails of shimmering dust suspended in the air, creating an atmosphere both ancient and otherworldly.

At the far end of the chamber stands a grand set of gold baroque gates, impossibly ornate despite their size. Elaborate curling motifs of leaves, wings, and swirling filigree shimmer faintly in the low crystal light. The metalwork looks alive, each curve and flourish meticulously crafted, every surface reflecting a warm, molten glow. The gates tower nearly to the ceiling, flanked by tall, dark metal pillars adorned with sharp-edged engravings and panels of hammered gold that catch every flicker of light.

In the center of the space, a dark elf woman stands in quiet, commanding poise. Her presence transforms the cavern, as though the environment itself bends toward her. Her skin glows with a cool moonlit sheen, contrasting with cascading obsidian hair that flows down her back like liquid night. Her eyes shine with an intense, focused light, reflecting the crystals around her. She wears armor and silk woven from metallic threads and spider-silk, the material catching the crystal glow in shifting patterns like constellations. Silver filigree wraps around her shoulders, and a necklace of carved crystal shards rests against her chest, each piece humming with faint magical energy.

Behind her, massive roots twist through the stone walls, gripping the cavern like the hands of an ancient giant. Some are wrapped in chains and metal braces, others fitted with gold ornamentation and arcane markings. Between the roots, metallic panels and dark iron frameworks form balconies and walkways, suggesting a hidden fortress or underground palace built layer by layer over centuries. Flickering braziers burn with cold blue flames, illuminating the narrow stairways carved directly into the rock.

The crystals continue to glow and pulse, their light reflecting off the gold gates and casting intricate patterns across the floor. The baroque metalwork seems to breathe with each pulse, shifting subtly with the cavern's heartbeat. The air is thick with ancient magic, metallic scents, and the faint vibration of power coursing through stone and crystal alike.
The entire underground scene feels like the throne chamber of an ancient and powerful dynasty, a place where light and darkness coexist in perfect equilibrium. It is a sanctum of secrets, a realm of quiet, awe-striking majesty, and the dark elf woman stands at its center like a living embodiment of its ancient power.

It feels alive. Breathing. Watching. A place where the spirit of the forest gathers in quiet harmony, where time slows, and where the natural world reveals its hidden, luminous soul.
```

### P0006. 氣體巨行星浮空都市（單段緊湊敘事）

```
An immense city floats in the upper atmosphere of a swirling orange and emerald gas giant, suspended on colossal anti-gravity platforms that glow like miniature suns. Towering crystalline skyscrapers rise toward the stratosphere, their surfaces reflecting storms churning below. Razor-thin bridges of shimmering energy connect the towers, humming with blue plasma. Thousands of small spacecraft zip between the structures like fireflies, leaving luminous contrails that bend in the wind currents. Beneath the city's suspended floor, lightning flickers through the gas giant's clouds, illuminating colossal vortexes that twist like planetary serpents. The entire scene feels impossibly delicate and overwhelmingly vast at the same time, a civilization perched on the edge of a living storm.
```

### P0007. 雲海平台獨影與巨環（分行長敘事）

```
A lone figure stands on a narrow platform high above a futuristic city, partially obscured by thick lavender and rose tinted clouds. The person appears small against the immense scale of the environment, wearing a long dark coat that hangs straight and still, suggesting a moment of quiet observation rather than movement. Their posture is upright and resolute, facing forward toward the city beyond.
In front of them, a colossal circular structure dominates the scene, resembling a massive window, portal, or architectural frame suspended in the air. Through this ring, a dense skyline of towering skyscrapers stretches into the distance. The buildings are tall and sleek, packed closely together, with glowing windows and illuminated accents in warm golds and cool teals. Light reflects softly off metallic surfaces, creating a layered sense of depth and scale.
The city appears to be at dusk or dawn, with a warm light source low on the horizon, casting a gentle glow between the towers. Atmospheric haze and mist soften the edges of the buildings, while the clouds swirl around the circular structure, partially veiling its upper and lower edges. The contrast between the warm city lights and the cool pastel fog gives the scene a dreamlike, otherworldly quality.
Above the ring, heavy structural elements and suspended machinery hint at advanced technology or a megastructure that supports or contains the view. Small details like vents, panels, and subtle lights suggest industrial complexity without drawing focus away from the central vista.
Overall, the image conveys a sense of awe and isolation. The lone figure appears to be contemplating the vast city ahead, framed by monumental architecture and drifting clouds, as if standing at the threshold between worlds or on the edge of a significant decision in a futuristic, cinematic setting.
```

### P0008. 環帶行星與冰晶碎塊（分行長敘事）

```
A vast cosmic landscape unfolds in layered depth, where a monumental ringed planet dominates the scene, its surface softly illuminated along one edge while the rest fades into deep shadow. Its rings sweep outward in elegant arcs, composed not of smooth dust alone but of countless fragmented shards, many of them crystalline, catching light in sharp, intermittent flashes.
Around it, multiple planets drift at varying distances, suspended in a dense star field streaked with luminous gas and distant stellar clusters. Some glow faintly with reflected light, others burn with subtle halos, their edges shimmering against the darkness. The background is alive with color, deep blues and teals blending into warm oranges and distant bursts of light, creating a sense of both infinite scale and quiet motion.
Cutting through this cosmic expanse are vast formations of ice and crystal, floating like fractured continents. These are not smooth or uniform surfaces. They are highly irregular, deeply detailed, and sharply defined. Each slab and shard is uneven, with jagged edges, chipped corners, and layered fractures that give the impression of immense pressure and ancient breakage. The surfaces vary between rough, matte frost and glass-like translucency, where light penetrates and scatters inside the material.
Some sections appear almost carved, with natural embossing formed by cracks, ridges, and subtle elevations. Fine fissures spread across the surfaces like veins, while thicker fractures create bold separations between planes. Edges catch starlight and glow in cold cyan tones, while deeper sections fall into shadow, emphasizing the relief and depth of the textures.
In the foreground and mid-distance, a field of floating particles drifts through space, like suspended frost or cosmic dust. These particles shimmer softly, forming gentle waves and currents that echo the earlier sense of motion. They gather in denser bands, almost like luminous mist, then disperse into finer grains that sparkle briefly as they catch light from nearby stars and planets.
The interaction of light defines the entire scene. Warm highlights from distant stellar sources brush against the cold ice, creating a contrast between golden reflections and icy blue translucency. The crystalline structures refract and diffuse this light, producing subtle internal glows and shifting tones that change depending on angle and thickness.
The composition feels both dynamic and suspended in time. Planets drift slowly, particles float in delicate currents, and the massive ice formations remain steady, like ancient structures frozen in place. Every surface carries detail, every edge contributes to the depth, and every material reflects a balance between energy and stillness.
It is a universe shaped by contrast, where warmth meets cold, smooth curvature meets fractured geometry, and vast emptiness is filled with intricate, tactile detail.
```
