### 【水墨水彩】冰湖極光

> 一點透視的廣角遠景：深夜墨色與海軍藍層層壓暗畫面，一道高飽和的克萊因藍＋極光綠水彩橫掃漆黑天空，冰面反射冷光——示範「單一句話也能含構圖、色盤、氛圍、質感四層資訊」。

```
An epic long shot ink and watercolor painting. in the center of a vast frozen lake, captured in a stark one-point perspective. The environment is enveloped in heavy, deep midnight-black ink washes and navy blue tones. Across the pitch-black sky, a fluid, highly saturated streak of brilliant Klein blue and electric aurora-green watercolor slashes dynamically through the darkness. The icy ground reflects this cold, vibrant glow. High contrast, sharp ink diffusion textures, intensely cold and mystical atmosphere, highly detailed.
```
