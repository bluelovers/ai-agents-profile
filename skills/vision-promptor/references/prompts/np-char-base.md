### 【人像・外型】東亞女性角色基底

> 同一份角色設定的兩種寫法：**形式 A** 以流暢句子描述，**形式 B** 以標籤群組表達——
> 兩者內容完全對應（五官／膚質 → 髮型 → 穿環 → 刺青），可互相轉換。
> 用途：作為「角色外型模組」的基底範本；需要種族替換時參見 [np-char-demi-human.md](np-char-demi-human.md)。

#### 形式 A：自然語言敘述版

```
An adult East Asian woman with pale matte skin, a slim V-line jaw, small nose, and dark almond eyes framed by heavy black winged liner and smoky shadow.
She has long dark brown hair with a soft fringe.
Her face features striking body modifications including a bridge piercing, septum ring, nostril stud, twin lower-lip rings, and dermal studs on her high cheekbones.
Thin single-needle black tattoos adorn her body, including a tiny star under one eye, fine-line artwork on her thigh, ornamental filigree running up the center of her belly to her sternum, and delicate script at her hip.
```

#### 形式 B：標籤分行版（同內容）

```
adult woman, East Asian face, pale matte skin, slim V-line jaw, small nose, dark almond eyes, long dark brown hair, soft fringe,

heavy black winged liner, smoky eye shadow,
bridge piercing, septum ring, nostril stud, snake bites lip piercing, cheekbone dermal studs,

star tattoo under eye, fine-line tattoo on thigh, ornamental filigree tattoo on belly and sternum, small script tattoo on hip,
```

> ⚠ 形式 A 用 `twin lower-lip rings`、形式 B 用 `snake bites lip piercing`——
> 同一部位的不同說法，示範標籤式可用業界慣用詞取代冗長描述。
